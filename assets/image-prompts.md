# Image Prompts

Единая система визуалов для одноэкранного MVP Pesticides RU. Все изображения сгенерированы встроенным imagegen и перенесены в `assets/img/`.

Общий стиль: Swiss-modern agro-industrial editorial illustration; off-white technical plate; subtle grid; deep green, graphite, muted amber, restrained red accent; vector-like low-relief 3D; no readable text, logos, watermark, smiling people, photorealistic stock fields, decorative leaves or exact agrochemical claims.

## assets/img/image-overview.png

System overview: crop protection, liquid micronutrients and agronomic service connected as one calm schematic composition with crop rows, sprayer silhouette, vial shapes and linked nodes.

## assets/img/image-products.png

Products catalog: rectangular product containers, seed kernels, droplets, leaf cross-section shapes and category blocks arranged as a technical catalog plate.

## assets/img/image-yantari.png

Yantari micronutrients: amber liquid droplet, laboratory vial, seed treatment tray, leaf tissue diagram and mineral node shapes.

## assets/img/image-schemes.png

Crop schemes: seed-to-vegetation crop-stage timeline with intervention windows, sprayer path, droplet and shield symbols.

## assets/img/image-services.png

Services: sprayer boom silhouette, lab glassware, soil sample tray, seed treatment drum and planning board connected by schematic lines.

## assets/img/image-company.png

Company architecture: blank central module connected to four blank modules, crop rows, lab node, product containers and route lines, without letters or pseudo-logo marks.

## assets/img/image-contacts.png

Contacts: abstract regional map fragments, simple pin circles, communication nodes, field parcel rectangles and request-routing lines.
